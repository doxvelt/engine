Mara, the ferry captain, and Nell, the quay keeper, are at the near-bank landing. Corin, a late passenger, has arrived carrying a sealed letter addressed to the old infirmary across the river. Nell has lowered the departure lantern. All three can see the letter, the lowered lantern and the approaching weather. :canonical
This is the last ferry before a storm. The boat is still tied up and usable; gusts are strengthening, and delay makes the crossing less safe. There is no other crossing available tonight. No passenger is already aboard. :canonical
Mara needs Nell's help with the shore ropes to leave. The quay has a mooring post a few steps from the boarding plank where two people could speak apart. No private exchange has happened. :canonical
The old infirmary provides care and keeps handwritten accounts. :canonical
The sealed letter corrects a laundry debt wrongly charged to Nell's sister Ada. Corin copied the wrong account number and has written a signed correction accepting responsibility. The letter contains no medical emergency. :canonical :hidden
@corin arranged to deliver his correction to the infirmary accounts clerk at the blue side door before the clerk leaves tonight. He fears public exposure will cost him the copying work that pays his rent. He has not opened the sealed letter since signing it. :canonical :hidden
@nell learned from Ada this afternoon that the blue side door serves accounts, while urgent care uses the front bell. Ada plans to wait outside that side door tonight for a correction promised by an unnamed clerk. Nell does not know who made the mistake or what is inside this letter. :canonical :hidden
@mara has felt the steering rope catch under load today. It still works, but she would have to tend it closely on a crossing in gusts; she has not told Nell or Corin. :canonical :hidden
