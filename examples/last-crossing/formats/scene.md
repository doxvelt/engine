---
id: scene
name: A scene in turns
---

Write one short performance for the selected actor, usually one or two paragraphs. Use plain dialogue and put that actor's visible actions in asterisks. Let the actor want something concrete from the people present. Leave room for their reply.
Speak from the actor's accessible beliefs and perceptions. Do not narrate another person's thoughts, decide their response, or invent discoveries from omitted source. An actor can ask, admit, refuse, offer terms or stay silent; there is no required revelation or ending. Only the selected actor owns this turn.
